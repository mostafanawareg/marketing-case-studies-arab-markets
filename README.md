# Marketing Case Studies in Arab Markets (36 cases, 4 sectors)

A structured dataset of 36 marketing case studies across four sectors — pharmaceutical (11), real estate (8), banking (8) and beverages (9) — compiled by **Dr. Mostafa Nawareg**, International Marketing Consultant, Corporate Trainer & Author.

Each row codes one case into the same analytical structure: the problem, the strategic decision, the generalizable lesson, the framework dimension it illustrates, and the strength of the evidence behind it.

## Columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Unique ID: sector prefix (PH, RE, BK, BV) + sequence number |
| `sector` | Pharmaceutical, Real estate, Banking or Beverages |
| `case_name` | Brand, company or case title |
| `problem` | The marketing problem or challenge as framed in the case |
| `strategic_decision` | The strategic move, reframing or decision taken |
| `lesson` | The generalizable managerial lesson |
| `framework_dimension` | The dimension of the sector framework the case illustrates (e.g. Name Power, Value Power, Arena, Channel) |
| `evidence_type` | Source and strength of evidence (case material, documented campaign, field observation, official company source, client testimonial); unverified claims are flagged |
| `source_url` | The full Arabic case-study article on mostafanawareg.com |

## Notes on evidence

- Medical and therapeutic statements in the pharmaceutical cases come from the original case material and are **not clinical claims**; they require independent scientific and regulatory verification.
- Reported results that conflict or lack independent data (e.g. the Augmentin case's 300% vs 430% first-month growth) are flagged in `evidence_type` rather than presented as fact.

## Suggested uses

Teaching and training (case-based learning), qualitative coding exercises, text classification of strategy types, and cross-sector comparison of marketing levers.
